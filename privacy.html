Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

Remote Business Partner ("RBP", "we", "us") is a recruitment provider helping employers recruit staff and candidates find suitable roles. This policy explains how information submitted through this website is handled.

2. Information we collect

We may collect applicant contact details, CVs, professional links and cover notes; candidate interest details; and employer contact and role requirement information.

3. How we use information

We use information to assess applications, communicate about recruitment, introduce suitable candidates where appropriate, respond to employer requests, and maintain recruitment records. We do not sell personal information or use automated recruitment decisions in this application.

4. Disclosure

Relevant candidate information may be shared with a prospective employer for a genuine recruitment purpose. Information may also be processed by service providers used to operate and secure the platform, including hosting, storage and authentication providers.

5. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We retain recruitment records for as long as reasonably necessary for our services and legal obligations.

Pre-launch requirement: the specific retention period must be confirmed before this policy is relied upon to collect real candidate or employer data.

7. Access, correction and complaints

You may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information, request deletion where appropriate, or raise a privacy complaint.
